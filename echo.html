(01) — UX DESIGN · ARTIST PLATFORM

ECHO — artist discovery, built for connection.

An immersive platform concept that unifies artists, producers, and labels, helping emerging musicians build their network, share their work, and get credited and paid for every collaboration.

ROLE
Product Manager
TIMELINE
2026 · Iteration 02
TOOLS
Figma, FigJam
PLATFORM
iOS Mobile

OVERVIEW

A home base for independent artists.

ECHO is an immersive platform concept for artists, built around discovery, connection, and a cohesive visual identity. Many are finding that they are left without resources, connections, and opportunities to kickstart their journey in breaking through the industry.

This project focuses on the unification of artists, musicians, producers, record labels, and other individuals active in the field. Through this, users will have an accessible opportunity to build their network, share their work, and overall, make the artist discovery process easier for professionals in the industry.

CASE STUDY

From scattered to structured.

01

Problem

With the number of independent artists on the rise within the music industry, many are finding themselves unsure of how to capitalize on the use of varying social media platforms to promote their music, make connections, and, overall, kick-start their journey breaking into the industry.

02

Research

Consolidated with a combination of personal creative practice, observation of emerging artists, and analysis of early-stage artist development workflows via social media such as TikTok and Instagram.

03

Direction

Same bones as the first iteration, re-skinned in pastel blues. Every surface now has a path into a credited, paid collaboration, and a shared split bar keeps every collaborator looking at the same truth.

04

Projected Outcomes

Help emerging artists define their identity earlier; reduce misalignment between sound, visuals, and branding; support more efficient collaboration between artists, producers, and creative teams; and give A&R teams a structured starting point for development conversations.
