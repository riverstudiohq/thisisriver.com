About
River

I'm Welcome! My name is River Ramirez, and it is a pleasure to be able to share my work with you! I am a product designer focused on turning user research into clear, accessible user experiences. My work spans mobile and web products, from problem solving to hi-fi prototyping.

I study Integrated Design and Media at NYU Tandon, where I explore the intersection of design, marketing, technology, and user behavior.

Core
Capabilities

Product Design & UX

End-to-end product design, user research, wireframing, and hi-fi prototyping. I translate insights into intuitive flows with clear information hierarchy and accessible interaction patterns.

Product Strategy

Problem definition, feature prioritization, and outcome framing. I structure case studies around user needs, business goals, and measurable impact.

Prototyping & Design Systems

Interactive Figma prototypes and component-based design systems that accelerate iteration and handoff. I build prototypes stakeholders can click through, not just look at.

User Research

Observation, competitive analysis, and synthesis of qualitative insights. My process grounds design decisions in real user behavior, whether it bein healthcare, music, or consumer mobile.

Front-End Implementation

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for responsive, production-quality interfaces. I understand implementation constraints and design with engineering feasibility in mind.

Business and Marketing

I integrate design, storytelling, and marketing strategy to build cohesive brand experiences. My work aligns visual systems, messaging, and audience engagement across digital platforms,from artist campaigns to product launches.
